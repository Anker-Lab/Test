About Kirkeibyen

We believe that Kirkeibyen's existence is justified by giving value to all people and creating space for a life-transforming encounter with Jesus and with other people.

Because people have value, we create space for meaningful relationships and engage with the city, the country and the nations. Because people have value, we offer relevant evangelism and equipping. Because people have value, we continuously position ourselves for change and growth. In this way, we deliver our mission best.

In all that we do, in all our relationships and wherever we are, we strive to live out these values:
