We use cookies on Kirkeibyen’s website to ensure that the site functions properly.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used for the technical functionality of the site.

2. Cookies we use

Necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be disabled.

Statistics (if enabled)

If we use analytics tools, cookies are used to collect anonymized data about website usage.
This is always voluntary, and you will be informed if this is the case.

3. Third parties

We only use third-party services when necessary for functionality or analytics.

4. Consent

By using the website, you accept necessary cookies. If we introduce additional cookies, you will be informed.

5. How to disable cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Please note that parts of the website may not function correctly without necessary cookies.
