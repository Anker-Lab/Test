What is Move?

Move is Kirkeibyen's annual conference, where we gather across our locations. It's a weekend of community, vision and connection as a church.

We meet to share what we experience in our daily lives, strengthen relationships and be inspired by what we stand for as a community.

Move is not about one location — but about the entire Kirkeibyen united as one church.
