Romania Mission Trip

During the Easter holidays, a team of young people from Kirkeibyen traveled to Romania to visit the mission project Rescue Mission. There, we experienced how God used us through prayer, worship, and testimonies among the inmates in the prison. We had the opportunity to bring energy, time, and fellowship into their daily lives and hear their stories. At the same time, the trip gave us a deeper joy in serving others and strengthened the unity within our team.
